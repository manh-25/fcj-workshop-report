### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The company has created a positive, open, and highly supportive working environment for interns to learn and develop their skills. The friendly and professional atmosphere made me feel comfortable and confident throughout my internship.

**2. Support from Mentors / Team Admin**  
The mentors were always supportive and willing to answer questions and provide guidance whenever I encountered difficulties, even outside of working hours. Their enthusiasm and strong sense of responsibility helped me gain valuable practical knowledge and develop a proper professional mindset in a corporate environment.

**3. Alignment Between Work and Academic Major**  
Although I was not able to learn as much or as deeply as Software Engineering students who already have a stronger technical background and a wider application scope, the knowledge and exposure gained during the internship have been very helpful for my current academic direction as well as my personal projects. Moreover, I see the long-term applicability of these skills and knowledge in future semesters and beyond.

**4. Learning Opportunities & Skill Development**  
The program provided many opportunities for me to enhance my professional skills, especially in areas related to AWS services. This serves as an important foundation, helping me feel more confident in my academic and career orientation in the future. The continuous support from the mentors was a key factor in enabling effective learning.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The company culture is built on collaboration, mutual respect, and strong support among team members. Everyone was willing to coordinate and assist with shared tasks, creating a positive working environment where interns feel included and valued.

**6. Policies / Benefits for Interns**  
The policies and benefits for interns were clearly implemented in accordance with the program guidelines. In addition, interns were given opportunities to participate in internal training sessions and knowledge-sharing activities to further enhance their skills and understanding.

### Additional Questions
- What are you **most satisfied with** during the internship?  
What I appreciate the most is the positive learning environment, the dedicated support from the mentors, and the opportunity to access practical knowledge, especially related to AWS services.

- What do you think the company **can improve** for future interns?  
At certain times, information shared among related parties was not fully synchronized, which occasionally made it challenging for interns to respond promptly. However, the company and mentors proactively provided support and adjustments when issues arose. This is a minor point that could be further refined in the future.

- If you were to recommend this internship to friends, would you **encourage them to join**? Why?  
Yes. I would recommend this program because it provides a suitable environment for learning, gaining practical experience, and developing skills in a structured and supportive manner.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations
- Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?

I hope that communication among all related parties will continue to be improved and better aligned, helping interns become more proactive during the internship.

- Would you like to continue this program in the future?

Yes. I would be very happy to have the opportunity to continue participating in the program to further learn and develop myself.

- Additional comments

I would like to sincerely thank the company and the mentors for their continuous support, guidance, and encouragement throughout my internship.
