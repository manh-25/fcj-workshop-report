# AI/ML/GenAI Workshop on AWS

**Location**: AWS Vietnam Office  

**Time**: 8:30 AM – 12:00 PM, Saturday, November 15, 2025  


## **Event Objectives**

* Provide hands-on exposure to AWS **AI/ML services** for both traditional machine learning and generative AI.  
* Help participants understand end-to-end **ML workflows on Amazon SageMaker**.  
* Introduce **Generative AI capabilities on Amazon Bedrock**, including foundation models and agent-based architectures.  
* Demonstrate practical approaches to deploying **RAG-based AI applications** on AWS.  
* Increase awareness of **AI safety, governance, and content control** using Bedrock Guardrails.


## **Program Structure & Key Highlights**

### **Welcome & Workshop Introduction**

* Registration and networking session.  
* Overview of workshop agenda and learning goals.  
* Brief introduction to the **AI/ML ecosystem in Vietnam** and AWS’s role in supporting it.


### **AWS AI/ML Services Overview – Amazon SageMaker**

#### **Amazon SageMaker as an End-to-End ML Platform**

* **Data Preparation & Labeling**  
  * Dataset cleaning, feature engineering, and automated labeling workflows.  

* **Model Training, Tuning & Deployment**  
  * Managed training infrastructure  
  * Hyperparameter tuning  
  * Deployment options: real-time inference & batch processing  

* **Integrated MLOps Capabilities**  
  * Model versioning  
  * Monitoring and drift detection  
  * Automated retraining pipelines  

#### **Live Demo – SageMaker Studio**

* Unified ML development environment  
* Jupyter notebook integration  
* Experiment tracking  
* Visual workflow builder  

→ Demonstrates how SageMaker simplifies the entire ML lifecycle compared to traditional local setups.


### **Generative AI with Amazon Bedrock**

#### **Foundation Models on Bedrock**

* **Claude, Llama, Titan** – model comparison

  * Use cases

#### **Live Demo – Building a GenAI Chatbot on Bedrock**

* Selecting and configuring a foundation model

## **Key Learnings**

* Amazon SageMaker provides a **complete ML platform**, significantly reducing operational complexity.  
* Choosing the right foundation model requires balancing **capability, cost, and specific use cases**.  
* **Prompt engineering is a core skill** that directly impacts output quality.  
* RAG is critical for GenAI applications that rely on **internal or frequently changing data**.  
* AI safety and governance must be **designed from the beginning**, not added later.


## **Applications to Work**

* Experiment with **SageMaker Studio** for internal data analysis and ML experiments.  
* Build **RAG-based GenAI applications** using Amazon Bedrock for internal documentation or knowledge systems.  
* Develop reusable **prompt templates and best practices**.  
* Integrate **Bedrock Guardrails** into GenAI applications to ensure safety and compliance.


## **Event Experience**

This was one of the most hands-on AI workshops I have attended.  
Building a chatbot using **RAG architecture on Amazon Bedrock** helped demystify many GenAI concepts that previously felt theoretical.

* SageMaker Studio offers an intuitive UI that simplifies ML lifecycle management compared to manual local setups.  
* The most impressive part was seeing **Bedrock Agents** coordinate multi-step tasks, resembling structured reasoning and decision-making.  
* Understanding Guardrails increased my confidence in controlling AI-generated content in real-world applications.

## **Key Takeaways**

* **RAG is the bridge between GenAI and enterprise data**: It enables AI systems to use internal knowledge without costly retraining.  
* **Prompt engineering is an art and a discipline**: Small changes can drastically alter outcomes.  
* **AI security and safety are non-negotiable**: Guardrails should be the first component, not an afterthought, in GenAI system design.
